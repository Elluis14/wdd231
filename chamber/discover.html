Site Plan – Radar Inmobiliario Juárez

Individual Project Site Plan – W05

1. Site Name

Proposed Site Name: Radar Inmobiliario Juárez

This name reflects a data-driven platform that provides a “radar” view of the real estate market in Ciudad Juárez. It suggests insight, analysis, and up-to-date information for buyers, sellers, and investors.

Optional domain idea: radarjuarez.com

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of the site is to present clear, visual, and data-based insights into the real estate market in Ciudad Juárez. The site will:

  • Show neighborhood-level data such as average home prices and price-per-square-meter.
  • Provide charts and simple dashboards with trends over time and basic indicators.
  • Offer forms for users to request personalized PDF or online reports about specific colonias.

3. Scenarios

The following questions represent typical visitors and help define the website content:

  • Scenario 1: “I want to buy a house in Juárez but I don’t know the city well. Which areas are more affordable and how have prices changed recently?”
  • Scenario 2: “I am an investor looking for rental property. Where can I see the best expected rental yield by area?”
  • Scenario 3: “Can I download or receive a report for one specific colonia with current average prices, trends, and basic risk indicators?”

4. Color Scheme

The color scheme is designed to feel professional, analytical, and trustworthy. The colors here are used in this document and will also be applied to the final site.

Primary – Navy Blue
Hex: #0b3c5d
Usage: header background, main headings, primary buttons or key links.
Accent – Emerald Green
Hex: #2f855a
Usage: accent buttons, highlighted labels, call-to-action elements.
Background – Light Gray
Hex: #f7f7f9
Usage: page background to separate content from the edges of the viewport.
Card – White
Hex: #ffffff
Usage: content panels, cards, data blocks, and sections with shadows.

In this site plan document, the header, headings, background, and section cards are already using the colors defined in this scheme.

5. Typography

Two main font families will be used to keep the design simple and consistent. The same fonts are already in use in this document.

Main Body Font

Font: system-ui, Segoe UI, sans-serif
Usage: body text, paragraphs, navigation, lists, labels, and small UI text.

Heading Font

Font: Georgia, serif
Usage: main page titles (<h1>) and section headings (<h2> and <h3>) to give the interface a more editorial and trustworthy tone.

In this site plan, the header, section titles, and subheadings use the Georgia serif stack, while the body text uses the system UI stack, demonstrating how typography will look on the final site.
